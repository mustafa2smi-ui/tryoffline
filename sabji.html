❓ Help & FAQs (Madad)

🛒 Sabji Order Kaise Karein?

  1. Apni Location ka tab select karein.
  2. Sabji card me `+` aur `-` button se kg choose karein.
  3. Niche floating **"View Order"** bar par click karein.
  4. **"WhatsApp Order"** par click karte hi order text send ho jayega.

🏪 Sabji Wale Register Kaise Karein?

Top-right me **Vendor Portal** button daba kar apni Dukan aur Location details submit karein. Owner approval ke baad aap rates upload kar sakte hain.

💰 Payment Kaise Hogi?

Payment direct sabji wale ko delivery ke waqt Cash ya UPI se karein. Portal par koi advance charge nahi lagta.
